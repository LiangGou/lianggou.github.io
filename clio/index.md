Clio is a personal AI assistant that its owner runs for his own use. It helps him manage information and get everyday work done through a private chat interface: answering questions, keeping notes and reminders, and — after he connects his Google account — working with his Gmail mailbox at his direction: checking and summarizing unread mail, searching messages, reading threads, and drafting replies for his review.

Clio is a single-user application. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and only its owner can connect an account. The Gmail connection is optional and exists so the owner does not have to sign in to Google separately for each session: he connects once, and can disconnect at any time from Settings > Connectors inside the app, or revoke access from his Google Account.
